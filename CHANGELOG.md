# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-27

Initial public web release.

- Singles, doubles, and three-player matches.
- 8-ball, 9-ball, 10-ball, and straight pool.
- Live scoring, breaker selection, undo/redo, and match duration.
- Continue matches and match history.
- Pick existing players or create new ones.
- Light and dark themes, blue and green colors, and text scaling.
- On-device storage for players, matches, and settings.
- Works offline after the first load.
